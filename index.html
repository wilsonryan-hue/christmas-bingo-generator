Pick a list, pick a grid, and print as many different bingo cards as you need, with a caller sheet to tick off as you play. Free, no sign-up, nothing saved. This is one self-contained file: save it and open it offline, or host it anywhere.

How to play

  • Give everyone a card and a pen, or counters such as buttons or dried pasta.
  • The caller reads the words from the caller sheet in order, ticking each one off.
  • Players mark any word they have. The first to complete a full row, column or diagonal shouts Bingo.
  • Check the winner's squares against the words ticked on the caller sheet.

Tip: the more words you give beyond the number of squares, the more different each card is. For a 5 by 5 grid with a free space you need at least 24 different words.
